# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-02

First release of The Human Odyssey, a browser survival and evolution game inspired by
*Ancestors: The Humankind Odyssey*.

- Procedural 1.2 km world: jungle, savanna, swamp, lake with river, cliffs and beach;
  day/night cycle, weather with rain, water and sky shaders, adaptive quality.
- Hominid clan with procedural characters and animation: knuckle-walking, running,
  jumping, tree climbing to the canopy, swimming, dodging, attacking, sleeping.
- Intelligence mode with sight, smell and hearing; identification of items, plants,
  animals, water and six unique landmarks; fear, dopamine and panic with the
  overcome-fear challenge.
- Survival: hunger, thirst, energy, health; bleeding, poison, fractures, cold and
  exhaustion with plant cures; drinking, eating, sleeping at the settlement.
- 28 items, 16 plants, 16 animal species with behaviour AI (predators that stalk and
  telegraph attacks, prey, territorial and neutral animals, nocturnal hyenas, eagles).
- Crafting: alterations and recipes (grinder, sharpened stick, chopper, opened coconut).
- Neuronal network with 40 neurons in six branches, reinforcement and genetic inheritance.
- Clan: grooming, baby carrying, outsider recruitment, mating, switching the controlled
  hominid; generation change with mutations and evolution leaps driven by 25 feats;
  lineage from 10 to 2 million years ago.
- Save/continue, settings (quality, volume, sensitivity, inverted look), English and
  Russian localisation, contextual hints, procedural audio.
- 238 unit tests, deterministic Playwright gameplay suite, visual smoke spec, CI with
  GitHub Actions.
